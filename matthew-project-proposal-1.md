## **Core Features**

The software development project aims to create a robust online platform for managing office catering subscriptions, focusing on seamless weekly rotation of cuisine types and flexible order management.

### 1\. Subscription and Payment System

This feature will handle the recurring nature of the catering service.

* **Weekly Subscription Model:** Offices subscribe to a recurring weekly catering service.

* **Automated Billing:** Implement a system for automatic weekly or monthly billing, linked to the office's chosen payment method.

* **Budget Management:** Allow the office to enter and preset a maximum budget for each catering event. The system must track the cost against this limit and flag when an order exceeds the budget.

### 2\. Office Profile and Requirements Management

The platform must capture and maintain essential information for fulfilling catering orders accurately.

* **Dietary Restrictions Database:** Allow the office manager to enter comprehensive dietary requirements (e.g., vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, specific allergies) that must be considered for all menu planning and order fulfillment.

* **Catering Needs/Materials:** A field for the office to list necessary materials or equipment for the catering service (e.g., disposable plates, cutlery, warming trays).

### 3\. Menu Planning and Delivery Schedule

This section outlines the process for menu creation and notification.

* **Thematic Weekly Cuisine Rotation:** The system's core logic will ensure a new cuisine type is delivered each week, driven by a predetermined schedule.

* **Cultural Celebration Integration:** Menus will be provided based on upcoming cultural celebrations, enhancing the weekly theme.

* **Two-Week Advance Menu Preview:** The full menu for a given week must be available for the office manager to view at least two weeks prior to the delivery date.

### 4\. Order Customization and RSVP Workflow

The office manager requires flexible options for finalizing the weekly order.

* **Manager Self-Selection:** The office manager can directly select menu items and quantities based on the pre-provided two-week-ahead menu, without requiring individual feedback from staff.

* **Internal RSVP System:**

  * The system must allow the office manager to send out an RSVP link/invitation to all registered office staff for the upcoming menu.

  * Staff can use the RSVP to indicate their preferred menu items and any specific dietary requests.

  * The platform will aggregate these responses, allowing the office manager to finalize the order based on the collected preferences and the set budget.

  * A deadline for staff RSVP submissions must be configurable.
